About The BlackBook

The BlackBook helps you find out what's happening in your city. It's a small business directory with a focus on Black-owned businesses — a place to locate businesses, products and services, and to shop The Black Market, our online store.

The BlackBook and The Las Vegas Blackbook app are marketing and networking tools created by EMC Consulting to help small businesses and startups get found, grow their customer base and connect with each other.
